Starting a conversation

What should I include?

A few details make it easier to understand your needs and suggest a useful next step.

Your goal

Tell me what the website should help your organization or audience do.

Your audience

Describe who will use the site and what information they need most.

Scope and timing

List the pages or features you have in mind and any important dates.

Examples

Share links to sites, styles, or existing materials that help explain your direction.

A typical next step

First, understand the problem.
Then choose the right solution.

A useful project starts with a clear brief. From there, the page structure, design direction, and build can be agreed on before the work moves forward.
